Privacy Policy

Net Sayacım — IF22 Software — Effective: 2026-06-24

This privacy policy applies to the Net Sayacım app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by IF22 Software (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download, register for, and use it. This information may include:

  • Your account details, such as your name and email address, provided when you sign up
  • The exams you select, your onboarding preferences, and the target scores you set
  • Your practice exam (deneme) entries, including subject results and calculated net scores
  • Your topic study progress (learned / needs work / not studied)
  • Basic device and usage information, such as device type, operating system, and in-app activity

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features. Score and ranking estimates are produced from historical, publicly available exam score tables and are approximations only.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information and required notices. With your consent, the Application may send you local reminder notifications from your device; you can disable these at any time from the device or in-app settings.

Third Party Access

The Application uses third-party services to operate. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data. The Application relies on the following providers:

  • Google Firebase — used for account authentication and secure storage of your app data
  • Cloudinary — used to store and serve the question photos you capture
  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements to users on the free plan
  • RevenueCat — used to manage subscription status for the Pro plan

Only the data necessary to operate the Application is transmitted to these services. The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with their trusted service providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information disclosed to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

Advertising

On the free plan, the Application displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use a device advertising identifier together with app usage and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy and advertising policies.

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization at any time from your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Subscribing to the Pro plan removes all advertisements.

Subscriptions & Purchases

The Application offers an optional Pro subscription (monthly or yearly). Purchases are processed by the platform stores (Apple App Store or Google Play), and the Service Provider does not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat using an anonymized user identifier so that your Pro features work across your devices. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace. You can also delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the Application (Settings → Delete Account).

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. If you would like the Service Provider to delete User Provided Data that you have provided via the Application, you may delete your account in-app, or contact them at if22dev@gmail.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.
