FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Pawfect Time free?

Yes. Pawfect Time is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start right away. The Apple Account on your device is your identity.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud account. There is no developer server. You can permanently erase everything in Settings > Data > Delete all data.

How do I share with my family?

Tap the "add person" button on a pet's profile to send an iCloud invite. The other person needs their own Apple Account and the app. You can both complete tasks and see who did what.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. The app is available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish and French.

Does it give veterinary advice?

No. Pawfect Time is a record-keeping and reminder tool and does not replace advice from your vet.
